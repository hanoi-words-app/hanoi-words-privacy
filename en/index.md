# Privacy Policy (Hanoi Words)

Last updated: 2026-11-19

[日本語](../)

"Hanoi Words" (the "App") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the App handles.

## 1. We do not collect personal information
The App **does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location**.

- The App has no servers of its own.
- There is no account registration or sign-in.
- The App communicates outside your device only to show ads (Section 3) and, when iOS needs it, to download voices for reading English words aloud.

## 2. Information kept on your device
To keep your study records, the App stores the following only on your device. It is never sent off the device.

- **Words studied**: the English words you answered and whether you were right.
- **Review schedule**: when each word should be reviewed next.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. To serve ads, measure impressions, and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as:

- **Device information**: model, OS version, language, etc.
- **Network information**: IP address and the approximate region inferred from it.
- **Ad records**: that an ad was shown or tapped.

The App does not ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). The advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used without your permission.
See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 4. Sharing with third parties
We never sell your information. Apart from the service in Section 3, we do not share it with third parties.

## 5. Deleting data
Deleting the App from your device erases all information the App stored on it.

## 6. Children
Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages so that junior high and high school students can use the App. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 7. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy as needed and will announce important changes in an app update or on this page.
